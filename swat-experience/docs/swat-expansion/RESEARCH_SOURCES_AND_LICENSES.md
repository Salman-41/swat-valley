# SWAT — Research catalog & source-rights guide

32 verified discovery links. Discovery is **not** the same as downloaded/approved content. No media is bundled as falsely cleared.

## Production rules
- CC0 or Met public domain: can generally republish with proper provenance; verify each exact downloadable item.
- CC BY: provide credit, link and license; mark edits. CC BY-SA: derived image adaptations may require ShareAlike. Do not assume all resulting site code must be ShareAlike.
- CC BY-NC: exclude from a portfolio promoting commercial services unless permission is granted.
- Research-only or permission-required: paraphrase facts with references; LINK to originals, do not download/rehost media or 3D/video.
- OSM geographic **data** terms and OSM public **tile server** terms are different.
- When a museum object is generally Gandharan rather than confidently Swat-sourced, label region uncertainty.
- When using archaeological dates, maintain source + uncertainty + publication date.
